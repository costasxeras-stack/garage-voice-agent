Evals

Scripted test calls played through the same pipeline. Each layer gets its own score, and a release gate says whether this version could ship. The test calls are synthetic: I wrote them, no real callers.

Speech errors swap words for common mishearings (brake to break, four to for) or drop them. With Rules, a noisy run repeats each call with 10 fixed random seeds and pools the results, because one seed can hide a miss. With Claude, one seed is used to keep the cost down, and each turn is one API call: up to 53 calls for the holdout set.

How it works
One page, no server. The garage, its prices and its bookings are made up.

  
The pipeline for one caller turn

    
Speech to texttoInput guardrailstoIntenttoSlotstoPolicy and toolstoWording (optional, Claude)toOutput guardrailstoText to speech

    
Blue steps are platform rules shared by every agent. Speech uses the browser's built-in recognition and voices. Claude is optional and only does understanding and wording.

  

  
Decisions

    
    
      
  • The model understands, the rules decide. In a regulated or safety-relevant call, what the agent does (hand off, verify, book) must be predictable and testable. Claude reads messy speech better than rules, so it fills the intent and slots. The policy that acts on them stays in code.
    • 
      
  • Safety rules always win. If the rules see a safety risk and the model does not, the call is still handed to a mechanic.
    • 
      
  • No booking details before the caller is verified with the reference and the number plate. Two wrong plates and the call goes to a person.
    • 
      
  • Prices are always estimates, and the agent never says a car is safe to drive or diagnoses it over the phone.
    • 
      
  • Confirm before acting. The agent books, moves or cancels only after a yes.
    • 
      
  • Free browser speech first. It costs nothing and works in Chrome, Edge and Safari. Voice quality and accuracy are lower than paid speech APIs, which is why the evals include simulated speech errors.
    • 
    

  

  
What is real and what is simulated

    
    
      
  • Real: speech recognition and voices (your browser), the Claude calls (your key), every timing shown, and every eval score, computed when you press Run.
    • 
      
  • Simulated: the garage, its prices, opening times and bookings, the tools (they read and write sample data in the page), and the handoff (the call ends with a message).
    • 
      
  • The test calls are synthetic and were written by the same person who wrote the rules. Scores without speech errors are an upper bound, not a measure of real calls.
    • 
    

  

  
Privacy
No transcript leaves your browser in Rules mode. In Claude mode the text of each turn goes to Anthropic's API with your key. Card numbers, emails and phone numbers are removed before anything else happens. Your browser's speech recognition may use its maker's servers (Chrome does).
